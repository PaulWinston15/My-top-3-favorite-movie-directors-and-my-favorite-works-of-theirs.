Steven Spielberg

![Spielberg](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0c53030c-cf5b-4fa7-b255-8cf12a7081db)

Steven Spielberg is my favorite movie director because even in modern cinema. Many of his movies feel timeless. It is like he is keeping cinema alive. 
His films aren't always as flashy as Christopher Nolan, whom I have a problem with due to his obsession with the soundtrack. Nor is he as arrogant as Nolan and James Cameron. When I watch a Spielberg movie, I feel like I'm watching an epic.
In modern cinema where CGI is all of the craze and controversy, I find myself enjoying more of Spielberg's movies that have more practical effects. His movies feel like a perfect balance between realism and surrealism where he doesn't try to overwhelm the viewer in one way or another. Even with a movie like Jurassic Park, many of the dinosaurs featured in the movie were life-sized animatronics and the actors were reacting to it in real life.
Even outside of movies where he is only an executive producer, his influence is still felt as shall be seen with my next favorite director.
When you watch a Spielberg film, you feel the realism sink in. That a lot of this could happen in real life. Steven Spielberg is the greatest movie director of all time.
